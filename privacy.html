Terminal Flow — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

Terminal Flow ("the game", on Google Play as "Terminal Flow: Airport Puzzle") is made by Steady Flow Studio, a one-person developer in Thailand. Contact: steadyflow.games@gmail.com

This page explains what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. Short version: the game has no accounts and never asks for your name, e-mail, phone number or location.

1. What stays on your phone

Your airport, progress and settings are saved on your phone only, including the name you may give your airport. We cannot see them. If you uninstall the game or clear its data, the save is gone (unless Android's own backup restores it).

2. Anonymous play statistics

To learn which parts of the game are fun or confusing, the game sends short records of what happens in play, for example "a zone was built", "a level was finished", "a session lasted 12 minutes".

  • Each record carries a random install number made by the game, the game version, the language, a rough screen size (phone / tablet) and the time. It does not carry your name, e-mail, contacts, location or anything you type.
  • The records go to a spreadsheet run by the developer (Google Apps Script / Google Sheets) and, in versions that include it, to Google Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports).
  • You can turn this off at any time: Settings → Play statistics. Turning it off stops sending and deletes the records still waiting on your phone.

3. Feedback you choose to send

The game has a Feedback box. Only when you press Send, the game sends:

  • the text and tags you wrote,
  • what the game looked like at that moment (airport, money, level, screen size, language, game version, and the phone model as your browser reports it),
  • only if you tick it: a small picture of the game screen and a copy of your game save (which includes the airport name you typed, if any), so the developer can reproduce a bug.

Please do not write personal details in the box. Notes (with the picture and save, when attached) are stored in the developer's Google Sheet and are used only to fix and improve the game.

4. Ads

The game shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one (for example to double a reward or to get an extra piece in a level). It never shows ads on its own. Ads are served by Google AdMob.

  • AdMob may use your phone's advertising ID, information about the device and its IP address (which gives a rough, city-level location, never your precise location) to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Where the law requires it (for example in the EU / UK), the game asks for your consent first.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, and turn off personalised ads, in your phone's Settings → Google → Ads (the exact menu depends on your phone).
  • How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

5. Purchases

If you buy the optional Supporter Pack, payment is handled by Google Play. We never see your card or bank details. We use RevenueCat to check that a purchase is valid and to restore it on a new phone; it receives an anonymous purchase record from Google Play, not your name or card.

7. Children

The game is not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

8. Security and how long we keep data

Everything is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Play statistics and feedback notes are kept only as long as they are useful for improving the game, and at most 2 years.

9. Your rights and how to delete your data

Under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and similar laws you may ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. To delete your data from Terminal Flow: Airport Puzzle (Steady Flow Studio):

  1. Play statistics: open the game → Settings → switch off Play statistics. Nothing more is sent, and the records still waiting on your phone are deleted at once.
  2. Your save (progress, airport name) lives only on your phone: uninstall the game, or clear its data in Android Settings → Apps → Terminal Flow → Storage.
  3. Everything we already received (play statistics, feedback notes with any picture or save you attached): e-mail steadyflow.games@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and roughly when you played or sent the note. We delete it within 30 days and reply when it is done.

What is deleted: play statistics, feedback notes, attached pictures and saves. What is kept: nothing that we hold. Google (AdMob, Play) and RevenueCat keep their own records under their own policies (see section 6); a purchase record stays with Google Play so the Supporter Pack can be restored. Without a request, play statistics and feedback notes are kept at most 2 years.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
